An evidence-first reading list on **diffusion / flow generative models × optimal transport (OT)**: 30 sub-topics in six sections,
from theory (is diffusion secretly OT? Schrodinger bridges, convergence rates) through flow matching and trajectory straightening,
cross-domain translation and modalities, to OT variants and solver infrastructure. Every paper comes with:

- a **deep-dive report** in Chinese (`reports/`, 8 fixed sections: problem / method / theory / numbers with sources / position on the map / limitations / takeaways / resources);
- the **original PDF** (`papers/`) and a **layout-preserving Chinese translation** by [SuperTranslate](https://github.com/asimfish/super_translate) with object-level QA (`papers_zh/`, `*.inspect.json`);
- machine-readable metadata (`data/`); this README is generated by `src/generator.py`.

Totals: 446 papers (⭐ core 139) | 438 deep-dive reports | 363 original PDFs | 40 Chinese translations. Evidence levels: [P] proceedings / [A] officially accepted / [R] preprint / [B] book or survey. Main conference, journal, workshop and preprint are always labelled separately.

Synthesis: [`report/`](report/) (problem → theory → classics → frontier → what we can do; zh/en PDF) and [`slides/`](slides/) (HTML deck + Beamer PDF). Q3-2026 trend scan: [`trends/`](trends/).

### T01. OT Mathematical Foundations

Topic digest: [`topics/t01.md`](topics/t01.md) (cross-paper observations / open problems / reading order) · topic note: [`source/kb/t01_ot_foundations.md`](source/kb/t01_ot_foundations.md)

1. ⭐ **Optimal Transport for Machine Learners.** arXiv 课程讲义, 2025. [B] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06589) [report](reports/2505.06589.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2505.06589.pdf)

    *Gabriel Peyré*

    480-page ML-oriented OT textbook: Monge/Kantorovich, Brenier, Sinkhorn, gradient flows, flow matching and transformers, with POT-based code.

2. ⭐ **Computational Optimal Transport.** Foundations and Trends in ML, 2019. [B] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.00567) [report](reports/1803.00567.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/1803.00567.pdf)

    *Gabriel Peyré, Marco Cuturi*

    Standard computational-OT textbook: discrete OT, duality, Sinkhorn, semi-discrete, Benamou-Brenier dynamics, barycenters, with three-layer exposition and code.

3. ⭐ **Optimal Transport for Applied Mathematicians.** Birkhäuser, 2015. [B] [paper](https://math.univ-lyon1.fr/~santambrogio/OTAM-cvgmt.pdf) [report](reports/Optimal_Transport_for_Applied_Mathematicians.md)

    *Santambrogio*

    Applied-math OT reference with rigorous proofs of duality, Brenier, Benamou-Brenier, and Wasserstein geometry

4. ⭐ **A computational fluid mechanics solution to the Monge–Kantorovich mass transfer problem.** Numerische Mathematik 84, 2000. [P] [paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002110050002) [report](reports/A_computational_fluid_mechanics_solution_to_the_Mo.md)

    *Benamou & Brenier*

    W2² equals minimal kinetic energy under continuity equation, giving a dynamic fluid formulation of OT.

5. ⭐ **Polar factorization and monotone rearrangement of vector-valued functions.** Comm. Pure Appl. Math. 44(4), 1991. [P] [paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.3160440402) [report](reports/Polar_factorization_and_monotone_rearrangement_of.md)

    *Brenier*

    Quadratic-cost optimal maps are unique convex gradients, the basis for diffusion-latent-as-OT claims

6. **A Survey on Optimal Transport for Machine Learning: Theory and Applications.** IEEE Access 13, 2025. [B] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01963) [report](reports/2106.01963.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2106.01963.pdf)

    *Luis Caicedo Torres, Luiz Manella Pereira, M. Hadi Amini*

    An application-focused OT survey for ML readers covering history, duality/entropic regularization math prerequisites, and five application areas; no new theory or experiments.

7. **Optimal and Diffusion Transports in Machine Learning.** arXiv 综述, 2025. [B] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06797) [report](reports/2512.06797.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2512.06797.pdf)

    *Gabriel Peyré*

    Informal survey unifying flow matching, OT, Wasserstein gradient flows and transformer PDEs via the continuity equation and non-unique velocity fields.

8. **Recent Advances in Optimal Transport for Machine Learning.** IEEE TPAMI 47(2), 2025. [B] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16156) [report](reports/2306.16156.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2306.16156.pdf)

    *Eduardo Fernandes Montesuma, Fred Ngolè Mboula, Antoine Souloumiac*

    TPAMI survey of OT for ML 2012-2023 across supervised, unsupervised, transfer and RL, plus computational OT extensions (sliced, low-rank, neural, minibatch, unbalanced) with a complexity table.

9. **A Combinatorial Algorithm for Semi-Discrete Optimal Transport.** NeurIPS, 2024. [P] [paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/2d950a2cfd8a75124c178a89545b97fd-Abstract-Conference.html) [report](reports/A_Combinatorial_Algorithm_for_Semi_Discrete_Optima.md)

    A combinatorial algorithm path for semi-discrete OT, distinct from smooth dual/Newton methods

10. **Lectures on Optimal Transport.** Springer UNITEXT 169, 2024. [B] [paper](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-031-76834-7) [report](reports/Lectures_on_Optimal_Transport.md)

    *Ambrosio, Brué & Semola*

    Lecture notes from SNS giving two self-contained proofs of Kantorovich duality

11. **Statistical optimal transport.** arXiv 讲义（Saint-Flour）, 2024. [B] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.18163) [report](reports/2407.18163.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2407.18163.pdf)

    *Sinho Chewi, Jonathan Niles-Weed, Philippe Rigollet*

    Saint-Flour lecture notes on statistical OT: Chapter 1 is a compact rigorous primer (Brenier via cyclical monotonicity, duality); Chapters 2-8 cover rates and Wasserstein geometry.

12. **An Invitation to Optimal Transport, Wasserstein Distances, and Gradient Flows.** EMS Textbooks, 2023. [B] [paper](https://ems.press/books/etb/258) [report](reports/An_Invitation_to_Optimal_Transport_Wasserstein_Dis.md)

    *Figalli & Glaudo*

    A 146-page minimal rigorous OT textbook covering duality, Brenier, Wasserstein distances, JKO/Otto calculus, with solved exercises.

13. **Optimal transport: discretization and algorithms.** Handbook of Numerical Analysis 22, 2021. [B] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.00855) [report](reports/2003.00855.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2003.00855.pdf)

    *Quentin Merigot, Boris Thibert*

    Handbook chapter giving self-contained convergence analyses of auction, Sinkhorn-Knopp and semi-discrete (Laguerre-cell, damped Newton) algorithms via Kantorovich duality.

14. **Convergence of a Newton algorithm for semi-discrete optimal transport.** J. Eur. Math. Soc. 21(9), 2019. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.05579) [report](reports/1603.05579.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/1603.05579.pdf)

    *Jun Kitagawa, Quentin Mérigot, Boris Thibert*

    Damped Newton for semi-discrete OT converges globally linearly and locally at rate 1+alpha under Loeper/MTW cost condition and a weighted Poincare-Wirtinger inequality on the source.

15. **Optimal Transport: Old and New.** Springer Grundlehren 338, 2009. [B] [paper](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-540-71050-9) [report](reports/Topics_in_Optimal_Transportation.md)

    *Villani*

    Villani's 30-chapter treatise: general Kantorovich duality, displacement interpolation, Monge problem, regularity, Ricci curvature and gradient flows; a reference, not a first read.

### T02. Diffusion-OT Theory

Topic digest: [`topics/t02.md`](topics/t02.md) (cross-paper observations / open problems / reading order) · topic note: [`source/kb/t02_diffusion_ot_theory.md`](source/kb/t02_diffusion_ot_theory.md)

1. ⭐ **Diffusion models for Gaussian distributions: Exact solutions and Wasserstein errors.** ICML, 2025. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14250) [report](reports/2405.14250.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2405.14250.pdf) [zh-PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-zh-v1/2405.14250.zh.pdf)

    *Emile Pierret, Bruno Galerne*

    Closed-form reverse SDE/PF-ODE and discretizations for Gaussian data; exact W2 error decomposition; SDE robust to init error, Heun best ODE scheme.

2. ⭐ **An optimal control perspective on diffusion-based generative modeling.** TMLR, 2024. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.01364) [report](reports/2211.01364.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2211.01364.pdf) [zh-PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-zh-v1/2211.01364.zh.pdf)

    *Julius Berner, Lorenz Richter, Karen Ullrich*

    Time-reversed log-density solves an HJB equation; ELBO follows from the verification theorem; path-space KL view yields the DIS sampler for unnormalized densities.

3. ⭐ **On the Trajectory Regularity of ODE-based Diffusion Sampling.** ICML, 2024. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11326) [report](reports/2405.11326.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2405.11326.pdf) [zh-PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-zh-v1/2405.11326.zh.pdf)

    *Defang Chen, Zhenyu Zhou, Can Wang, Chunhua Shen, Siwei Lyu*

    PF-ODE sampling trajectories share a content-independent linear-nonlinear-linear shape in a ~3-D subspace; GITS DP time schedule cuts FID at 5-10 NFE.

4. ⭐ **Understanding DDPM Latent Codes Through Optimal Transport.** ICLR, 2023. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07477) [report](reports/2202.07477.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2202.07477.pdf) [zh-PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-zh-v1/2202.07477.zh.pdf)

    *Valentin Khrulkov, Gleb Ryzhakov, Andrei Chertkov, Ivan Oseledets*

    Conjectures the DDPM PF-ODE encoder equals the Monge OT map to N(0,I); proven for Gaussians, transport-cost error ~1e-15 on synthetic densities via tensor-train FP solver.

5. ⭐ **The flow map of the Fokker–Planck equation does not provide optimal transport.** Appl. Math. Lett. 133:108225, 2022. [P] [paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S089396592200180X) [report](reports/The_flow_map_of_the_Fokker_Planck_equation_does_no.md)

    *Lavenant & Santambrogio*

    Rigorous counterexample: the Fokker-Planck flow map is generally not the OT map to the standard Gaussian; obstruction is a Hessian non-commutativity; quantifying the defect left open.

6. **Learning Monge maps with constrained drifting models.** arXiv 2603.25182, 2026. [R] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25182) [report](reports/2603.25182.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2603.25182.pdf)

    *Théo Dumont, Théo Lacombe, François-Xavier Vialard*

    Constrained gradient flow of KL inside the convex cone of OT maps exists globally and converges to the Monge map at a dimension-free exponential rate; parameterized version is natural gradient descent (toy MMD 0.019 vs Adam 0.029).

7. **Adjoint Matching: Fine-tuning Flow and Diffusion Generative Models with Memoryless Stochastic Optimal Control.** ICLR (Spotlight), 2025. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08861) [report](reports/2409.08861.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2409.08861.pdf)

    *Carles Domingo-Enrich, Michal Drozdzal, Brian Karrer, Ricky T. Q. Chen*

    Memoryless noise schedule σ(t)=√(2η_t) is necessary for unbiased reward fine-tuning; Adjoint Matching solves SOC via least-squares regression without importance weighting

8. **Stochastic Optimal Control Matching.** NeurIPS, 2024. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02027) [report](reports/2312.02027.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2312.02027.pdf)

    *Carles Domingo-Enrich, Jiequn Han, Brandon Amos, Joan Bruna, Ricky T. Q. Chen*

    Casts stochastic optimal control as least-squares regression with a path-wise reparameterization trick for variance reduction; lowest L2 error on 3 of 4 control problems.

9. **The Brownian transport map.** Probab. Theory Relat. Fields, 2024. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.11521) [report](reports/2111.11521.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2111.11521.pdf)

    *Dan Mikulincer, Yair Shenfeld*

    Constructs a transport map via the Föllmer process from Wiener measure to Euclidean target measures, proving almost-sure contraction where OT map contraction is open, yielding new functional inequalities and Stein kernels

10. **Understanding Diffusion Models by Feynman's Path Integral.** ICML, 2024. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.11262) [report](reports/2403.11262.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2403.11262.pdf)

    *Yuji Hirono, Akinori Tanaka, Kenji Fukushima*

    Path integral formulation of diffusion models; WKB expansion computes NLL for noisy sampling

11. **Wasserstein proximal operators describe score-based generative models and resolve memorization.** arXiv 2402.06162, 2024. [R] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06162) [report](reports/2402.06162.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2402.06162.pdf)

    *Benjamin J. Zhang, Siting Liu, Wuchen Li, Markos A. Katsoulakis, Stanley J. Osher*

    SGMs are Wasserstein proximal operators of cross-entropy; kernel model with HJB terminal condition avoids memorization

12. **Formulating Discrete Probability Flow Through Optimal Transport.** NeurIPS, 2023. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03886) [report](reports/2311.03886.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2311.03886.pdf)

    *Pengze Zhang, Hubery Yin, Chen Li, Xiaohua Xie*

    Continuous PF is a Monge map on finite intervals for collinear data; a one-directional ReLU generator defines discrete probability flow, cutting CIFAR-10 CSD from 57.7 to 9.4.

13. **Score-based Generative Modeling Secretly Minimizes the Wasserstein Distance.** NeurIPS, 2022. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06359) [report](reports/2212.06359.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2212.06359.pdf)

    *Dohyun Kwon, Ying Fan, Kangwook Lee*

    W2(data, generated) <= C sqrt(J_SM) + I(T) W2(p_T,q_T): the score-matching loss upper-bounds the Wasserstein distance via time-derivatives of W2 along continuity equations.

14. **Comparison of transport map generated by heat flow interpolation and the optimal transport Brenier map.** Comm. Contemp. Math. 23(6), 2021. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06464) [report](reports/1709.06464.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/1709.06464.pdf)

    *Anastasiya Tanana*

    Kim-Milman heat-flow transport map generally differs from the Brenier map: explicit Gaussian counterexample with drift -Ax, numerical one for standard Gaussian.

15. **Score-Based Generative Modeling through SDEs.** ICLR (Oral), 2021. [P] [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=PxTIG12RRHS) [report](reports/Score_Based_Generative_Modeling_through_SDEs.md)

    *Song et al.*

    Unifies diffusion models via SDEs, proposing reverse SDE and PF-ODE

<a id="t03"></a>
### T03. Schrodinger Bridges for Generation

Topic digest: [`topics/t03.md`](topics/t03.md) (cross-paper observations / open problems / reading order) · topic note: [`source/kb/t03_schrodinger_bridge.md`](source/kb/t03_schrodinger_bridge.md)

1. ⭐ **Light and Optimal Schrödinger Bridge Matching.** ICML, 2024. [P] [paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/gushchin24a.html) [report](reports/Light_and_Optimal_SB_Matching_LightSB_M_Gushchin_e.md)

    Single 'optimal projection' bridge-matching step recovers the SB from any coupling; Gaussian-mixture potential gives a CPU-minutes solver (LightSB-M).

2. ⭐ **Schrödinger Bridge Flow for Unpaired Data Translation (α-DSBM).** NeurIPS, 2024. [P] [paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/bb3cfcb0284642a973dd631ec9184f2f-Abstract-Conference.html) [report](reports/Schr_dinger_Bridge_Flow_IMF_DSBM_De_Bortoli_et_al.md)

    Continuous 'Schrödinger Bridge Flow' whose discretization gives alpha-IMF; for alpha<1 a single network is finetuned online and converges to the SB.

3. ⭐ **Diffusion Schrödinger Bridge Matching.** NeurIPS, 2023. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16852) [report](reports/2303.16852.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2303.16852.pdf)

    *Yuyang Shi, Valentin De Bortoli, Andrew Campbell, Arnaud Doucet*

    Iterative Markovian Fitting alternates Markovian and reciprocal projections to reach the SB; DSBM implements it with forward/backward bridge matching, preserving both marginals.

4. ⭐ **Likelihood Training of Schrödinger Bridge using FBSDEs Theory.** ICLR, 2022. [P] [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=nioAdKCEdXB) [report](reports/Likelihood_Training_of_SB_using_FBSDEs_SB_FBSDE_Ch.md)

    FBSDE theory turns SB optimality PDEs into an exact log-likelihood objective generalizing SGM's ELBO; alternate IPF-style training reaches CIFAR10 FID 3.01.

5. ⭐ **Diffusion Schrödinger Bridge (DSB), De Bortoli et al.** NeurIPS (Spotlight), 2021. [P] [paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/940392f5f32a7ade1cc201767cf83e31-Abstract.html) [report](reports/Diffusion_Schr_dinger_Bridge_DSB_De_Bortoli_et_al.md)

    Implements each IPF half-step as a learned time reversal with score networks; SGM is the first iteration; first SB solver at image scale.

6. **Diffusion & Adversarial SB via IPMF, Kholkin et al.** ICLR (Poster), 2026. [A] [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=38fGCBhFF5) [report](reports/Diffusion_Adversarial_SB_via_IPMF_Kholkin_et_al.md)

    Shows the practical bidirectional alternating IMF heuristic is an IMF+IPF combination (IPMF), proves convergence in several settings, exposes a similarity-quality knob.

7. **Reflected Schrödinger Bridge Matching.** arXiv 2607.03626, 2026. [R] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03626) [report](reports/2607.03626.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2607.03626.pdf)

    *Marcus Häggbom, Viktor Nilsson, Pierre Nyquist, Joakim andén*

    Extends alpha-DSBM to reflected Brownian reference on the unit cube via closed-form reflected score and bridge sampler; negligible overhead, samples stay in-domain.

8. **From Schrodinger Bridge to Optimal Transport over Sub-Riemannian Manifolds.** arXiv 2605.11429, 2026. [R] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11429) [report](reports/2605.11429.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2605.11429.pdf)

    *Daniel Owusu Adu, Karthik Elamvazhuthi, Bahman Gharesifard*

    Noise only along control directions yields a well-posed hypoelliptic SB under bracket-generating assumptions; strong duality and convergence to sub-Riemannian OT as noise vanishes.

9. **Foundations of Schrödinger Bridges for Generative Modeling.** arXiv 2603.18992, 2026. [B] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18992) [report](reports/2603.18992.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2603.18992.pdf)

    *Sophia Tang*

    220-page self-contained tutorial deriving static/dynamic SB, stochastic control, FBSDEs, Markovian/reciprocal projections and discrete SB as one framework for generative modeling.

10. **Categorical SB Matching (CSBM), Ksenofontov & Korotin.** ICML, 2025. [P] [paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/ksenofontov25a.html) [report](reports/Categorical_SB_Matching_CSBM_Ksenofontov_Korotin.md)

    Proves D-IMF converges to SB on discrete finite state spaces, extending SB matching to discrete data

11. **Exponential Convergence Guarantees for Iterative Markovian Fitting.** NeurIPS, 2025. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20871) [report](reports/2510.20871.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2510.20871.pdf)

    *Marta Gentiloni Silveri, Giovanni Conforti, Alain Durmus*

    First non-asymptotic exponential convergence rate for IMF under log-concave and weakly log-concave marginals

12. **Feedback SB Matching (FSBM), Theodoropoulos et al.** ICLR (Oral), 2025. [P] [paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k3tbMMW8rH) [report](reports/Feedback_SB_Matching_FSBM_Theodoropoulos_et_al.md)

    Semi-supervised SB: under 8% paired samples enter a generalized EOT objective as state feedback; authors report faster training and better generalization.

13. **Momentum Multi-Marginal Schrödinger Bridge Matching.** NeurIPS, 2025. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10168) [report](reports/2506.10168.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2506.10168.pdf)

    *Aude Genevay, Gabriel Peyré, Marco Cuturi*

    Trains generators by backpropagating through L Sinkhorn iterations on minibatches; proves the Sinkhorn loss interpolates between OT (eps to 0) and MMD (eps to infinity).

5. ⭐ **Sinkhorn Distances: Lightspeed Computation of Optimal Transportation Distances.** NeurIPS, 2013. 7. **Energy-guided Entropic Neural Optimal Transport.** ICLR, 2024. [P] [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.06094) [report](reports/2304.06094.md) [PDF](https://github.com/asimfish/awesome_diffusion_OT/releases/download/pdf-en-v1/2304.06094.pdf)
